Expat Tax Relief Schemes

🇳🇱 Netherlands 30% Ruling

Qualifying foreign specialists recruited from >150km outside the Dutch border receive 30% of their gross salary tax-free for up to 5 years.

🇩🇰 Denmark Researcher Scheme

Flat 32.84% tax rate (including labor market contribution) for highly-paid key employees and researchers for up to 7 years.

🇪🇸 Spain Beckham Law (Special Expat Regime)

Flat 24% tax rate on income up to €600,000 for up to 6 consecutive tax years for relocating engineers and executives.

The 5-Step Tech Relocation Roadmap

From selecting your destination to landing at the airport with a stamped work visa and furnished apartment key.

1

Destination & Official Sponsor Research

Identify target countries based on language barrier, tech ecosystem, and visa timelines. Consult official government registers (e.g. Netherlands IND list, UK Gov sponsor register) to ensure companies hold valid sponsorship licenses before applying.

2

ATS Optimization & LinkedIn Geo-Targeting

Strip complex multi-column layouts, graphics, and photos from your CV. Use the Google XYZ formula (Accomplished [X] measured by [Y] by doing [Z]). Switch LinkedIn "Open to Work" location to your target country (e.g. Amsterdam, Berlin, London) to appear in local recruiter searches.

3

High-Signal Recruiter Outreach & Applications

Target talent acquisition managers and engineering directors directly with a concise 3-sentence note outlining years of experience, primary stack, and immediate availability for visa relocation. Prioritize verified visa platforms like Relocate.me, Honeypot, and Landing.Jobs.

4

Technical & Behavioral Interview Mastery

Deliver sharp 5-minute introductions. Frame behavioral answers with the STAR method. Approach take-home assignments with the MoSCoW framework: ship clean, fully tested core functionality (TDD) rather than over-engineering unfinished features.

5

Package Negotiation, Visa Filing & Relocation

Ensure the offer contract includes flight reimbursements, 30-60 days temporary furnished apartment, settling-in allowance, and visa filing fees for dependents. Obtain apostilles for diplomas and marriage/birth certificates early while embassy processing proceeds.
